**About Charnwood Eco Hub CIC**

![Sarah Goode, Emma Ward and Clive Bagley](assets/img/charnwood-eco-hub-directors.jpg){:width="75%"}

Charnwood Eco Hub CIC was founded by friends Emma Ward and Sarah Goode, who
share a passion for protecting the environment and creating practical ways
for local people to live more sustainably. Having both been involved in
environmental and community projects across Charnwood for many years, they
recognised the need for a central hub where people could access resources,
learn new skills, and connect with others who care about the planet.

Today, Charnwood Eco Hub brings together a range of community-led
environmental initiatives under one roof, including our
[Scrapstore](/projects/scrapstore), [Library of
Things](/projects/library-of-things), [Refill
Station](/projects/refill-station) and [community
workshops](/workshops-and-events). Through these services, we help people
save money, reduce waste, reuse resources, repair rather than replace, and
gain the skills and confidence to live more sustainably.

We're also delighted to have Clive Bagley as a Director. Clive spent over
30 years as a director at DIAM, an international manufacturer of bespoke
point-of-sale solutions, bringing extensive leadership, strategic and
commercial experience to the organisation. A passionate maker and lifelong
tinkerer, Clive leads the development of our [community
Makerspace](/projects/makerspace), helping create a welcoming space where
people can repair, create, learn new skills and share knowledge, extending
the life of everyday items and inspiring creativity within the community.

Together, our board combines expertise in community development,
sustainability, governance and business, ensuring Charnwood Eco Hub
continues to grow as an accessible, welcoming space that enables people to
take practical action for both the environment and their local community.
