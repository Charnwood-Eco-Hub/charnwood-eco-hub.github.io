  <h1>Accessibility</h1>
</div>
<div class="grid__item">
Charnwood Eco Hub has moved to 7-8 Baxter Gate, Loughborough LE11 1TG. We are
on the ground floor, and now have accessible toilet facilities. We also have a
parking space which we can reserve for customers with mobility issues on
request. There are bus stops right outside and the nearest public car park is
at Beacon Bingo hall.
