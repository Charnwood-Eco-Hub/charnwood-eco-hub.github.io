Charnwood Eco Hub is a not-for-profit, shared community hub based in Loughborough. Our aim is to provide a space to meet, make, fix, create and share sustainable ideas and resources.

We're excited to announce that Charnwood Eco Hub is moving to a new home in the heart of Loughborough! From Saturday 1st August, you'll find us at:

7–8 Baxter Gate
Loughborough
LE11 1TG

You'll be able to browse our Scrapstore, refill your household essentials, borrow useful items from our Library of Things, discover our growing range of sustainable products, and take part in workshops and community events.

This move marks an exciting new chapter for Charnwood Eco Hub. Our new location is in a central, more accessible location, enabling us to develop new activities, and welcome even more people to be part of our mission to reduce waste, share resources and inspire sustainable living across Charnwood.

To celebrate, we'll be holding an Opening Day from 10am to 2pm on Saturday 1st August. Come along to explore the new Hub, meet the team, find out more about our services, and see what's planned for the months ahead. Whether you're already a member or visiting us for the first time, we look forward to welcoming you.

We'll continue to expand what we offer over the coming months, including our refill range, workshops, and Makerspace activities, so keep an eye on our website and social media for the latest updates.

Thank you to everyone who has supported us so far. We can't wait to welcome you to our new home at 7–8 Baxter Gate!
