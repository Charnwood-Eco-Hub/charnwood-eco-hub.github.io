Charnwood Eco Hub is a not-for-profit, shared community hub based in Loughborough. Our aim is to provide a space to meet, make, fix, create and share sustainable ideas and resources.

  • Explore our Scrapstore
  • Browse our Library of Things
  • Discover our sustainable cleaning and personal hygiene products
  • Find out about our workshops and courses
  • Find out how you can volunteer and get involved
  • Meet the team and find out more about what we’re working on

New Opening Hours

From Monday 2nd November 2026, we’re making a few changes to our opening hours!

Our new opening hours will be:

🗓️Wednesday: 4pm–8pm
🗓️Thursday: 10am–2pm
🗓️Saturday: 9am–5pm

You’ll still be able to pop in for creative supplies at our Scrapstore, borrow something from our Library of Things, visit our refill station, explore the Makerspace or find out about our workshops.

We look forward to welcoming you in during our new opening hours! Thank you for supporting your local community hub and helping us make reuse, creativity and sustainability accessible to everyone.
