# Kids' Halloween Macrame Monster Keyring Workshop

*Get creative this Halloween at Charnwood Eco Hub!*

Kids can learn some simple macramé techniques and make their very own spooky
keyring, choosing from a skeleton, pumpkin man or monster design. It’s a fun,
hands-on activity and a great chance to make something unique to take home. The
workshop is £5 per child and runs as a drop-in session from 11am–1pm on
Saturday 31st October. An adult must stay with each child and booking is
required.

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click
here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/halloween-macrame-monster-keyring-workshop).
