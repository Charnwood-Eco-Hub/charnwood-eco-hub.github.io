# Charnwood Eco Hub Rag Rug Taster Workshop

Sat 24th October, 11am-1pm. £15/£10 per person

*Come along to Charnwood Eco Hub for a relaxed, hands-on introduction to the wonderful world of rag rug making.*

You’ll learn some of the basic techniques for turning old fabrics and textiles into a colourful, practical rag rug, while having a go yourself and picking up tips along the way. It’s a great way to get creative, learn a new skill and give unwanted textiles a new lease of life - no previous experience needed. All materials and equipment will be provided, so just bring yourself and get ready to make something!
