# Charnwood Eco Hub Thermal Cooker Making Workshop

Join us on Wednesday 30th September 2026 from 4.30pm-7.30pm at Charnwood
Eco Hub, 7-8 Baxter Gate, Loughborough, LE11 1TG and learn how to make a
simple, low-cost thermal cooker using upcycled and everyday materials.

Thermal cookers use retained heat to continue cooking food without
electricity or gas, helping you save energy, reduce your carbon footprint,
and create delicious slow-cooked meals.

*Leave with your own thermal cooker, practical tips, and recipes to try at home.*

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click
here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/thermal-cooker-making-workshop).
