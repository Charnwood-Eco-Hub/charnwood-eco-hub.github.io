# Charnwood Eco Hub Sari Necklace Workshop

Join us on Wednesday 16th December 2026 at Charnwood Eco Hub, 7-8 Baxter
Gate, Loughborough, LE11 1TG from 5.30pm-7.30pm and learn how to make your
very own, lovely, hand sewn, unique necklace, using beautiful colourful
sari fabric. All materials provided. £10/£15.

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click
here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/sari-necklace-workshop-6a5135ea353520-70898945).
