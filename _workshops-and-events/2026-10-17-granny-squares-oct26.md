# Granny Squares Crochet Workshop

Sat 17th October, 11am-1pm, £15/£10 per person

*Learn the timeless craft of crochet and create your own colourful granny
squares in this friendly, relaxed workshop.*

You’ll learn the basic crochet stitches and techniques needed to make a classic
granny square, with plenty of guidance and support along the way. Perfect for
complete beginners or anyone wanting to refresh their crochet skills, you’ll
leave with your own handmade squares and the inspiration to turn them into
something useful and beautiful.

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click
here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/crochet-workshop)
