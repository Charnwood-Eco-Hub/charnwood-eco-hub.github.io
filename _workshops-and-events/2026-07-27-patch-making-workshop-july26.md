# Charnwood Eco Hub Patch Making Workshop

Join us on Monday 27th July 2026 from 11am-1pm at Charnwood Eco Hub, SOFA Building, Clarence Street, Loughborough, LE11 1DY and give old fabrics a new life!

*Design and create your own unique upcycled patch using reclaimed materials to customise clothes, bags or accessories.*

A fun, creative workshop that's perfect for beginners. All materials included. Suitable for age 12 upwards (must be accompanied by an adult)

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click
here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/patch-making-workshop).
