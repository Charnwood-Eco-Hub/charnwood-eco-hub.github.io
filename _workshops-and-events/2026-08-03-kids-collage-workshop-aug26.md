# Charnwood Eco Hub Kids Collage Workshop

Join us on Monday 3rd August 2026 from 2.30pm-4.30pm at Charnwood Eco Hub, 7-8 Baxter Gate, Loughborough, LE11 1TG for a fun-filled collage workshop where children can cut, stick and create their own unique masterpiece using recycled magazines, colourful papers, stickers and more. £5 per child. suitable for 8 years upwards (an adult must stay).

You can book your place by scanning the QR code on the poster or [click here](https://www.eventbookings.com/b/event/kid-s-collage-workshop).
