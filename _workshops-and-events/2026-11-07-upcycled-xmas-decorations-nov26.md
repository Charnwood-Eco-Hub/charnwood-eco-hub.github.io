# Charnwood Eco Hub Upcycled Paper Christmas Decorations

Sat 7th November, 11am-1pm. £15/£10 per person

*Get creative this Christmas while giving old paper a new lease of life!*

In this hands-on workshop, you’ll learn how to transform unwanted books, magazines, packaging and other paper into beautiful, unique Christmas decorations. A relaxed and creative session that’s perfect for making something festive, sustainable and a little bit different - with decorations to take home and enjoy for years to come.
