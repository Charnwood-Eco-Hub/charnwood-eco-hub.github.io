# Autumn 2026 Newsletter

**Welcome to the Autumn 2026 Edition of the Charnwood Eco Hub Newsletter!**

What a few months it’s been at Charnwood Eco Hub! As the seasons change, we’re
excited to be settling into our **new home in Baxtergate**. Our new premises are
giving us more space to share, make, learn and connect, and we’re looking
forward to seeing the Hub grow into an even bigger part of our local community.

We’ve also had some wonderful recognition for the work happening here, having
been named **finalists in the BBC Make a Difference Awards!** It’s a real honour to
have the Hub and the people who make it happen recognised in this way – and we
couldn’t have got here without our amazing volunteers, workshop leaders,
members and everyone who supports what we do.

And there’s plenty happening over the coming months. Our **autumn and winter
workshop programme** is now taking shape, with lots of opportunities to learn new
skills, get creative, make something useful from reclaimed materials and spend
time with other like-minded people. From cosy seasonal crafts and upcycling to
practical making sessions, there’s something for all ages and abilities. So
grab a cuppa, have a browse through what’s coming up, and we hope to see you at
the Hub soon!

# BBC Make A Difference Awards

![BBC Make A Difference Awards Team Photo](/assets/img/bbc-make-a-difference-awards.jpg){:width="75%"}

We were absolutely delighted to be named **finalists in the Environmental
category of the BBC Radio Leicester Make a Difference Awards** this year!

It was a real privilege to attend the awards ceremony and celebrate alongside
so many amazing people and community organisations from across Leicestershire
and Rutland.

What really stood out to us was the incredible range of things people are doing
to **make a difference** - from supporting vulnerable people and strengthening
communities, to protecting our environment, tackling inequality, inspiring
young people and so much more.

Being surrounded by people who are giving their time, energy and ideas to make
their communities better was a brilliant reminder of just how much fantastic
work happens locally, often quietly and without much recognition.

We’re incredibly proud to have been recognised as finalists for the work our
team is doing through **Charnwood Eco Hub** - reducing waste, sharing resources,
encouraging sustainable living and creating opportunities for people to get
involved in their community.

A huge congratulations to all the finalists, winners and everyone nominated.
And thank you to **BBC Radio Leicester** for shining a light on the wonderful
people and organisations making a difference across our region.

We’re proud to be part of it.

# Carbon Literacy Training

[![Carbon Literacy Project Poster](/assets/img/carbon-literacy-project-poster.png){:width="75%" target="_blank"}](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6BO19DUr4pVFT5PRuBKauQ7AdI_d0QoxSoPwBUw0LKog8KA/viewform)

Transition Loughborough, one of the groups who organised the People’s Emergency
Briefing, is in the process of applying for funding to run some Carbon Literacy
courses in early 2027. If you're interested please [fill out this short form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6BO19DUr4pVFT5PRuBKauQ7AdI_d0QoxSoPwBUw0LKog8KA/viewform){:target="_blank"}.

Carbon Literacy is a days’ worth of learning about climate change. We would be
using a version of Carbon Literacy specifically designed for those involved in
their communities. This idea has come about because it was mentioned on the
flipcharts at the People’s Emergency Briefing.

If you might be interested in attending a course (assuming Transition
Loughborough are successful in getting funding) please [fill out this short
form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6BO19DUr4pVFT5PRuBKauQ7AdI_d0QoxSoPwBUw0LKog8KA/viewform){:target="_blank"}

Filling out the form **does not** commit you to attending a course. **It will** help
Transition Loughborough to know how much interest there is likely to be and how
many times they should look to run the course.

# Autumn/Winter Workshop Updates

[![Upcycled Terrarium Workshop Poster](/assets/img/upcycled-jar-terrarium-oct26.png){:width="75%" target="_blank"}](/workshops-and-events/)

Our autumn and winter programme is packed with opportunities to **learn, make
and get creative** at the Hub, with workshops taking place on most Saturdays
throughout the season.

There’s a wide variety to choose from, including creative crafts, upcycling,
sustainable making and **special workshops for children**, so there should be
something for everyone. Many of our workshops make use of materials from our
[Scrapstore](/projects/scrapstore), giving unwanted and surplus materials a new
lease of life while keeping the cost and environmental impact down.

We also want our workshops to be accessible and affordable. Sessions are **£15
per person, or just £10 for Hub members, students and people on a low income.**
Your workshop fee supports our facilitators and helps us continue providing
activities and resources for the local community. We operate as a non-profit
and are run mostly by volunteers.

Whether you want to try a completely new skill, make something useful, meet
other creative people or simply spend a Saturday doing something a little
different, **come and join us at the Hub!** Check out our [upcoming workshops here](/workshops-and-events).
If you're interested in running a workshop with us, please do get in touch,
we're always interested in new ideas!

# Leicester Co-Operative Grocery

We’re excited to see **SPRoUT**, a new worker-owned grocery project taking root in
Leicester.

SPRoUT is being developed as a different kind of food shop – one focused on
**good-quality, everyday ingredients at fair prices**, with sustainability and
co-operative ownership at its heart. The planned shop will stock staples such
as rice, pasta, oats, pulses, flour, oils, herbs and spices, alongside fresh
fruit and vegetables and ecological household products. Wherever possible,
products will be available loose, helping customers reduce packaging and buy
exactly what they need.

The shop will be **owned and run by its workers**, with one member, one vote.
Rather than profits going to outside shareholders, any surplus will be
reinvested into the business, helping to keep food affordable and support the
people who work there. SPRoUT is inspired in part by the long-running Unicorn
Grocery in Manchester.

The project is currently looking for suitable premises in Leicester of around
300 square metres or more. The team has already developed its business model,
sourcing principles and ethical charter, and is now working on premises,
funding and building its founding worker-member group. There is **no confirmed
opening date yet.**

We love the idea of another local project making sustainable, low-waste choices
more accessible and helping to build a stronger local food economy. It’s
definitely one to watch!

You can find out more about SPRoUT and follow the project’s progress at
[sprout-grocery.co.uk](https://sprout-grocery.co.uk){:target="_blank"}

# The Fix It Group 23 Turns 3

[![Fix It Group 23 Turns 3 Poster](/assets/img/leicestershire-fixers-toolkit.jpg){:width="75%" target="_blank"}](https://thefixitgroup23.com/upcoming-events)

**You're Invited!**

This October, The Fix It Group 23 will be celebrating **three years of our Repair
Café**, and we're delighted that our anniversary event also falls on
**International Repair Day** on **Saturday 17th October.**

To mark the occasion, we'd love to invite you to join us for part (or all!) of
the day. Come along for a slice of cake, a cuppa and a chat with fellow repair
enthusiasts about all things repair, reuse and sustainability.

If you've got something at home that's in need of some TLC, feel free to bring
it along too – our fantastic volunteer repairers will be on hand to see if they
can give it a new lease of life.

Since opening our doors in October 2023, we've welcomed **1,670 items** through our
Repair Cafés. Around **70% have been successfully repaired**, helping to keep an
estimated **2.2 tonnes of household items out of landfill**. Along the way we've
built an amazing team of volunteers, shared skills, saved people money and
shown just how much can be achieved when a community comes together to repair
rather than replace.

This year also marks the **10th International Repair Day**, making it an even more
special occasion. We're pleased that our local MP, Peter Bedford, will be
joining us. Peter recently signed the **Repair and Reuse Declaration** following a
visit from members of our committee, demonstrating his support for extending
the life of products, reducing waste and promoting the Right to Repair.

We'll also be welcoming other Repair Cafés, community organisations and local
environmental groups, and we hope the day will be a fantastic opportunity to
celebrate everything that repair represents—sharing skills, reducing waste,
protecting our environment and bringing communities together.

The event will take place from **10:00am until 1:00pm, Saturday 17th October,
at MMC Venue, 105 Leicester Road, Mountsorrel.**
